Bio

I’m Dr. Li Jing, an Assistant Professor at Xiamen University Malaysia.

My background combines a PhD in Informatics Engineering with academic and industry experience across China, the United States and Malaysia. My interests span AI, networking, IoT, cybersecurity, digital twins and LLM applications.

Official university profile ↗

This research agenda connects machine-learning-based threat detection with trustworthy AI, secure agent architectures and human-supervised cyber defense. IoT, edge and cyber-physical environments provide the deployment context where reliability, privacy and resource constraints matter.

The goal is a connected progression from observing threats to explaining evidence, making decisions and taking accountable action.

Research agenda

Detect→Understand→Reason→Act

Protect the AI that acts.

DETECTION → OPERATIONS

Turn telemetry into evidence.

Connect intrusion detection and explainable outputs to evidence-grounded SOC agents, investigation and incident reporting.

AI SECURITY → AGENT SECURITY

Make autonomy defensible.

Bring guardrails, least privilege, isolated execution and runtime monitoring into agentic systems.

NETWORKS → THE EDGE

Test intelligence in context.

Study generalization, federated learning and robustness under the resource and telemetry constraints of IoT and CPS.
